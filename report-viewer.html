Work / 03 / HubSpot

Single Report Viewer: one platform for every report

HubSpot Reporting had many disconnected templates doing the same jobs in different ways. I set a vision and led design to consolidate them into one platform experience, cutting duplicate technical overhead and making room for new features like AI insights.

00 · The problem

Reporting was getting more powerful, and harder to use.

In 2022, every new feature was complex to add, and customer feedback showed it.

Usability

Disconnected systems

We added functionality to users' world without helping them navigate it.

Consistency

Many patterns, same job

Different templates solved the same jobs to be done in different ways.

Insight

No opinion

Users wanted more help finding meaning in their data.

01 · The vision

Flow, functionality and service.

01

Flow

Help users navigate complexity.

02

Functionality

Deliver new features that deepen users' understanding of their data.

03

Service

Build reusable components across jobs to be done that other teams can use.

Before designing, I defined success in behavior: more report saves, hovers and drilldowns, fewer trips to the report builder, more use of opinionated insights, and higher Reporting CSAT.

Internally, I mapped systems, pitched a vision deck to other teams, inventoried functionality to consolidate templates, and scoped how to deliver value faster. Externally, I gathered pain-point themes from NPS, customer calls and prior research, tested lo-fi ideas with customers, ran a Kano survey to prioritize, and validated hi-fi designs in user testing.

02 · Consolidating templates into a platform

Two building blocks every report can share.

I inventoried every report template, modeled the shared parts, and defined two building blocks.

Building block 01

Report controls

One set of controls and customization options available on both custom and out-of-the-box reports.

Building block 02

Report associations

Modular pieces (summary, chart, table, insights, metadata) that can be called into any space in HubSpot.

04 · Impact

More usage, higher satisfaction, more mature reporting.

Report viewer usage grew 32% year over year, to 79M unique events and 301k unique users in 2024. Reporting CSAT rose 2.85 standard deviations compared with the previous year. New interactive components drew 683k hovers and 99k drilldowns, and users saved 28k new reports, a sign of growing reporting maturity. Display options built into the viewer, such as forecasts, anomaly detection and trend lines, were adopted by more than 100k users and improved two-week retention by 30%.

What I learned

Build a resilient team.

Plan for what happens if nothing changes and if everything changes, and give the vision room to ride along with other initiatives.

Create spaces for collaboration.

Know when to use lo-fi and when to use hi-fi, and make shared documents people want to work in.

Services over spaces.

Make your team valuable to other teams and meet users where they work. It makes pivoting easier.
